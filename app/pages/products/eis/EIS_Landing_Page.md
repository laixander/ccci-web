- **Repeated layers:** "View all", "Synced", "Delayed", "Draft", etc. repeat throughout the CSV; I assigned each to its mockup by following the layout order.
- **Hub diagram labels:** The six module names appear twice in the CSV (once in the checklist, once as hub nodes), and "EIS CORE" is split across two lines in the source.
